Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

vortexapps collects minimal information necessary to operate our services. This includes usage data such as page visits, interaction logs, and technical information like browser type and IP address collected automatically. We do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., via the contact form).

2. How We Use Information

Information we collect is used exclusively to: improve and maintain our services, respond to your inquiries, analyze site performance, and prevent abuse. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain site functionality (e.g., theme preference, session state). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You may decline optional cookies through our cookie consent banner without loss of functionality.

4. Firebase & Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for real-time data synchronization and authentication. Firebase may collect usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. No sensitive personal data is stored in Firebase.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for up to 90 days for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. Usage analytics data is retained for up to 12 months. You may request deletion of your data by emailing beatwave790@gmail.com.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at beatwave790@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and report any suspected security issues to us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last modified" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at beatwave790@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
